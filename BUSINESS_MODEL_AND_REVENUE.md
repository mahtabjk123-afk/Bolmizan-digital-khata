# BolMizan Protocol — 100% Shariah-Compliant B2B2C Business & Revenue Blueprint

## 1. Core Philosophy: Free Forever for Farmers & Small Merchants
Rural agricultural markets (Sabzi & Grain Mandis) reject upfront subscription fees. BolMizan follows an **M-Pesa and Khatabook-inspired B2B2C model**:
- **100% Free Forever for End-Users:** Offline Khata, Sindhi/Urdu Voice Mizan, QIM Sabzi Parchi, and Adversarial Anti-Scam Shield cost **PKR 0** for farmers and small shopkeepers, fueling rapid zero-cost viral adoption.
- **Monetization at Ecosystem Scale:** Revenue is generated from institutional partners (Islamic banks, agri-input manufacturers, sugar/rice mills, and Takaful providers) once transaction volume scales.
